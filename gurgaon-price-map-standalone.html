How the range is built

For each project we take the midpoint of its quoted ₹/sq ft. A sector's range is the lowest to highest project midpoint; its colour is the median. With 3–4 projects that is a ballpark, not a valuation — one ultra-luxury launch (Krisumi Waterside in 36A, DLF Dahlias in 54) can pull a sector's top end far above its typical stock.

Ticket sizes in ₹ crore

Indicative only: the sector's ₹/sq ft range multiplied by a typical super area (2 BHK 1,350 · 3 BHK 1,900 · 4 BHK 2,800 sq ft by default — edit the boxes in the panel). Real units vary a lot in size, and PLC, parking, GST and club charges come on top.

What "same league" means here

We kept quotes from established branded developers and their premium/luxury lines. In mature sectors with no new launch (Old Gurugram, Sushant Lok, Golf Course Road) the evidence is resale asking rates in well-known condominiums, flagged resale. Use the chips to include or exclude those.

Search

The search box knows every quoted project plus every named residential society, condominium and neighbourhood OpenStreetMap has in Gurugram. A society resolves to the sector its footprint sits in and shows that sector's range; societies OSM only has near a point-mapped sector are marked "near". Sector prices are not resolved for societies with no quote — you get the location and the sector's ballpark.

Map data

"Entry to Delhi" markers are where NH-48, Dwarka Expressway, Old Delhi Road and MG Road cross the state line.
